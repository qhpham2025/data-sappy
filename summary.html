Meet Sappy: the Master Data Pet that keeps SAP B1 clean and grows sales

Bad master data is invisible, so nobody owns it. We gave it a face. Sappy's health is your data's health.

😿 The problem

  • Email-driven MDM: no standard form, so requests bounce; approvers don't see them; Amy's item code took 18 days.
  • Duplicates (“Natl. Univ. Hospital” vs “National University Hospital”) cause wrong invoices and distort regional PO history.
  • No audit trail; bank details travel by plain email.
  • IT needed for every new BU, field or approval step.
  • Forecasts use flat 6–12-month averages that miss seasonality, churn and cross-sell across A/B/C in 5 countries.
One root cause → two problems: dirty data in = slow operations and wrong commercial decisions.

🔮 How AI beats “Grandma's Average”

Today: flat 6-month average. Sappy: clean → learn seasonality pooled across all 15 country×product series → trend → re-season. Back-tested on Aug–Oct '26: WAPE 12.6% → 4.6%. Dec '26 forecast: 7,295 units vs 5,928 flat, so we avoid under-ordering at the peak. Automated monthly, replacing ~2 days of spreadsheet work per cycle*.

🤔 Why this, not the alternatives?

  • vs. tweaking the email process: no dedupe, no audit trail, still invisible.
  • vs. a custom portal: needs developers Lives Group doesn't have. Sappy runs on M365 they already own.
  • vs. an enterprise MDG suite: overkill and costly at SAP B1 scale.
  • Not in v1: auto-create without human check, LLM chatbot, ML dedupe, mobile app.
